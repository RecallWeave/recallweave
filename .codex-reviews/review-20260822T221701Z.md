## 1. Findings

### High — persisted edge identity and classification remain unauthenticated

[src/recallweave/contract.py](/Users/josh/particle-workers/recallweave/src/recallweave/contract.py:650) copies `is_verified`, `kind`, `score`, endpoints, and therefore `evidence_class` directly from the persisted `edges` row. The subsequent checks authenticate only the evidence payload:

- side passages against indexed sections;
- candidate terms against endpoint vocabulary;
- candidate `method` and `explanation` against constants.

They never establish that the edge itself is one the indexer could have produced.

The database schema only constrains `is_verified` to `0/1`; it does not constrain `kind`, score, endpoint ordering, or consistency among those fields ([index.py](/Users/josh/particle-workers/recallweave/src/recallweave/index.py:76)). The genuine producers are much stricter:

- authored edges use a parsed link kind, `is_verified=1`, and `score=1.0` ([index.py](/Users/josh/particle-workers/recallweave/src/recallweave/index.py:348));
- candidates use `kind="discovery_candidate"`, `is_verified=0`, and a recomputed cosine score ([index.py](/Users/josh/particle-workers/recallweave/src/recallweave/index.py:490)).

Concrete failure scenarios:

1. Insert an edge between two notes that share two indexed words, supply authentic candidate constants and optionally authentic section sides, but assign an arbitrary score. It passes every current gate and is exported as a genuine discovery candidate even if it falls below the indexing threshold or was never selected by the bounded top-per-note algorithm.
2. Change a candidate’s `kind` to `"human_verified"` or another fabricated string. It remains `evidence_class="discovery_candidate"` and exports the invented kind unchanged.
3. Insert an `is_verified=1` row with `{}` evidence and an arbitrary endpoint pair. Empty evidence satisfies the current `authored_link` applicability rules, so the artifact claims an authored, verified relationship without authenticating any source link.
4. Change an edge’s endpoint pair to another pair sharing the listed terms. Vocabulary checking succeeds, but the edge’s existence and ranking remain unauthenticated.

This is the fifth level: the candidate envelope and evidence sides are authenticated, but the persisted edge record that binds them and declares their class is not. It violates the repository’s verified/candidate evidence boundary and blocks promotion.

I did not modify a database to reproduce this because the review sandbox and brief prohibit working-tree writes. The acceptance path is directly visible at `contract.py:650-750`; there is no envelope validation or recomputation before `connections.append(candidate)` at line 759.
