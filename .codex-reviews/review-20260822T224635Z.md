### High — Heading-link re-derivation does not bind evidence to the claimed line

At [contract.py](/Users/josh/particle-workers/recallweave/src/recallweave/contract.py:386), `_matching_links_in_heading` validates:

- the source note has any section with the stripped heading text; and

It never uses `persisted["line"]`. The `sections` table stores body `line_start` and `line_end`, but not the heading’s physical line. Consequently, an authentic heading can authenticate an arbitrary forged coordinate.

Minimal in-memory reproduction against the current helper:

claimed line 999999 -> accepted
